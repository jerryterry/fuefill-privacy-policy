FUEFILL — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

FUEFILL ("the App") is a fuel-price and fuel-station information application. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use FUEFILL.

1. Location Information

FUEFILL may request access to your device's location to provide location-based features, such as finding fuel stations near you.

Location permission is optional. You can deny or revoke permission through your Android device settings.

FUEFILL does not intentionally request background location access.

2. Fuel Prices

FUEFILL may allow users to submit fuel-price information. Submitted information may include fuel type, fuel price, fuel-station information, date and time of submission, and location or approximate location associated with the submission.

This information may be used to display and compare fuel prices.

3. Device Information

Depending on the services integrated into the App, technical information may be processed, including device type, Android version, app version, diagnostic information, IP address, and advertising identifier.

4. Advertising

FUEFILL may display advertisements. If Google AdMob or another advertising service is used, that service may collect and process information according to its own privacy policy. Advertising providers may use advertising identifiers and similar technologies to provide and measure advertisements.

5. How Information Is Used

  • Provide fuel-station information
  • Find nearby stations
  • Display fuel prices
  • Compare fuel prices
  • Process user-submitted prices
  • Improve the App
  • Diagnose technical problems
  • Maintain security
  • Display advertisements
  • Comply with legal requirements

6. Sharing of Information

FUEFILL does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers necessary to operate the App, including advertising, hosting, mapping, analytics, database, or crash-reporting providers where applicable. Those providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy or as required by applicable law. Information that is no longer required may be deleted or anonymized.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

FUEFILL is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for purposes prohibited by applicable law.

10. Third-Party Services

FUEFILL may use third-party services for features such as advertising, maps and location, analytics, hosting, database services, and crash reporting. The privacy practices of those services are governed by their respective privacy policies.

11. Your Choices

  • Deny location permission
  • Revoke location permission
  • Change Android privacy and advertising settings
  • Stop using the App
  • Contact us about privacy questions

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's features, third-party services, or applicable legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at the Privacy Policy URL.
